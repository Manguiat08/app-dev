# My Favorite Series {#favorites}

## Taxi Driver (K-Drama)

**Taxi Driver** (*Mobeom Taxi*) is a Korean crime-action drama that aired on *SBS*, starring `Lee Je-hoon` as Kim Do-gi.

> A secret taxi company helps crime victims get the revenge the law couldn't give them.

### Quick Facts

1. Aired on SBS
2. Season 1 premiered in 2021
3. Season 2 followed in 2023

- Lee Je-hoon plays Kim Do-gi, the driver
- Kim Eui-sung plays Jang Seong-cheol, the company CEO
- The taxi company is called Rainbow Transport[^1]

## One Piece (Anime)

**One Piece** is an adventure anime by *Toei Animation*, based on the manga by `Eiichiro Oda`.[^2]

> Monkey D. Luffy sets out to find the legendary treasure and become the Pirate King.

### Some Story Arcs

| Arc          | Highlight                                                                                    |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| East Blue    | Luffy begins recruiting his crew                                                             |
| Alabasta     | The Straw Hats help save a kingdom with Princess Vivi                                        |
| Water 7      | The Going Merry, Robin's disappearance, and the crew's emotional struggles.                  |
| Marineford   | A massive war involving Luffy, Ace, Whitebeard, and the Marines.                             | 
| Wano         | The raid on Onigashima and Luffy's Gear 5 awakening                                          |
